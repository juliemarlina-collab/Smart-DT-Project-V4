Phase 01 · Empathy

Understand users before you solve.

In this phase, your team interviews, observes, listens and documents real user needs before moving to Define.

What is Empathy?

Empathy is the first phase of Design Thinking. Students go out into the real world to understand the people they are designing for — their feelings, struggles and real needs.

What students need to do

  • Prepare interview questions using T01 Interview Guide.
  • Conduct interviews with at least 3 real target users.
  • Record and transcribe interviews with permission.
  • Complete Empathy Map, Persona and User Needs Summary.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Do not ask leading questions such as “Don’t you think this is a problem?”
  • Do not interview only friends. Find real target users.
  • Do not assume what users feel without evidence.
  • Do not skip observation. Watch what users actually do.

T01 Interview Guide

Plan and conduct interviews with at least 3 real target users.

Not Started
Activity Introduction

What are you going to do?

You will prepare open-ended interview questions, conduct user interviews and capture observation notes so your team can understand the problem from the user’s point of view.

Minimum3 target users
Question typeOpen-ended
OutputQuestions + insights

A1What students need to do

Step 1: identify your target user group and interview context.

Step 2: prepare warm-up questions and 5 main empathy questions.

Step 3: conduct interviews, observe behaviour, and record 3 key insights.

Sample Walkthrough

Interview context: Canteen lunch queue

Target user: Diploma student who often buys lunch between classes.

Weak question

“Do you think the canteen queue is a big problem?”

Better question

“Tell me about your experience buying lunch during peak hour.”

What to notice: repeated complaints, waiting time, facial expression, body language, decision points and exact user quotes.

T02 Empathy Map

Organise evidence into Says, Thinks, Does and Feels.

Not Started
Activity Introduction

What are you going to do?

You will turn raw interview notes into a visual empathy map. This helps your team separate direct quotes, hidden thoughts, observed actions and emotions.

InputTranscript + notes
MethodSort evidence
Output4-quadrant map

A2What students need to do

Says: copy direct user quotes from the transcript.

Does: add observed actions and behaviour.

Thinks / Feels: infer carefully using evidence, not assumptions.

Sample Infographic

Empathy Map Example

Says

“The queue is too long.”

Thinks

“I might miss class.”

Does

Checks the time repeatedly.

Feels

Frustrated and rushed.

Reminder: Use exact evidence for Says and Does. Be careful when writing Thinks and Feels.

T03 Persona

Create one research-based persona from repeated patterns.

Not Started
Activity Introduction

What are you going to do?

You will combine findings from several users into one realistic persona. The persona should represent the common needs, goals, frustrations and behaviours of your target users.

InputPatterns from T02
FocusOne target user
OutputPersona profile

A3What students need to do

Build: a realistic user profile, not a random character.

Include: quote, goals, pain points, workaround and solution need.

Check: every persona detail must connect back to interview evidence.

Sample Persona Card

Aina, 20

T04 User Needs Summary

Summarise patterns, needs and evidence from Empathy research.

Not Started
Activity Introduction

What are you going to do?

You will convert all interview and mapping evidence into clear user needs. This prepares your team to write a stronger problem statement in Phase 02 Define.

Minimum3 interviews
Look forRepeated patterns
OutputUser needs summary

A4What students need to do

Group: repeated evidence from interviews and empathy maps.

Write: user needs with evidence, not solution ideas.

Prepare: submit the summary for lecturer/supervisor review.

Sample Needs Summary

From evidence to user need

Pattern

Students complain about long queues during short breaks.

Evidence

3 users mentioned waiting time and fear of being late to class.

User Need

Students need a faster way to decide where to buy lunch because they have limited break time.

Important: Do not write the final solution yet. Write what the user needs and why.

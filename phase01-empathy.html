T01 POEMS Observation Framework

Observe the real situation using People, Objects, Environment, Messages and Services.

Mini example: Situation: Students are confused while waiting at a service counter. People: students and counter staff. Objects: forms, phones and queue number. Environment: busy counter area.

T02 Interview Guide & Worksheet

Prepare questions and record important user responses as evidence.

Mini example: Question: What is difficult about this process? User answer: “I do not know which counter to go to because the instructions are unclear.”

T03 Audio-to-Text Transcription

Turn interview audio or notes into clear written evidence.

Mini example: Transcript quote: “I waited for 20 minutes because I was not sure if my form was complete.” Keyword: waiting, unclear instruction, incomplete form.

T04 Empathy Map

Organise user evidence into what users Say, Think, Do and Feel.

Mini example: Says: “I do not know where to queue.” Thinks: “I might submit the wrong form.” Does: asks friends for help. Feels: confused and worried.

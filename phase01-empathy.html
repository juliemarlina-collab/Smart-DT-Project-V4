PHASE 01 · EMPATHY

Understand users before you solve.

Use T01–T04 to collect evidence, listen to users and organise findings before Define.

🕵️ Empathy Rule for Beginners

You are a detective, not a doctor. Collect evidence first. Do not suggest a solution yet.

Quick Info: Empathy Phase

Start with the visual guide, then complete the four input templates in order.

Phase 01 evidence flow
T01 POEMS: Observe the first-time batik workshop visitor.T02 Interview: Ask the visitor about their experience.T03 Transcription: Capture the visitor's real words.T04 Empathy Map: Sort findings into Say, Do, Think and Feel.

T02 Interview Guide

Ask, listen, collect stories, actions, thoughts and feelings.

Common mistakeAsking leading questions.

Mini caseAsk what felt difficult before trying batik.

T03 Transcription

🎙️
Transcription turns audio into written evidence.
Keep their words. Lose the noise.
What to writeAudio info, speaker labels, clean transcript and key quotes.
Common mistakeChanging the user's meaning.
Enough when3–4 key quotes + 1 short insight.
Mini case“I was scared I’d spoil the cloth.”

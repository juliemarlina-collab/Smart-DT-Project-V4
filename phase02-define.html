What is Define?

Define is where your team makes sense of everything learned during Empathy. You analyse interviews, maps and personas to frame ONE clear problem statement that captures the real user need.

What students need to do

  • Review your Empathy Map, quotes and needs from Phase 01.
  • Build a User Persona (T05) from real evidence.
  • Cluster findings into insights and write a User Need Statement (T06).
  • Frame a Design Challenge using a How Might We statement (T07).
  • Pass the Define quiz, then submit to earn your badge.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Do not invent persona details — use real evidence.
  • Do not confuse a need with a solution.
  • Do not group notes by who said them — group by meaning.
  • Do not skip the insight: it explains why the need matters.

T07 Problem Framing & Design Challenge

Frame a How Might We question and define the design challenge that will guide your Ideation phase.

Quick Fact

A design challenge opens up possibilities — it is a question, not a solution. Start with "How Might We…"

Take your User Need Statement from T06 and reframe it as an open question your team can ideate around. A good HMW question is broad enough to allow many solutions but focused on the real user need.

Example: "How might we help craft sellers reach more buyers without leaving their stall?"

🧭 Define Rule for Beginners

You are a translator, not an inventor. Turn real evidence into one clear persona and one sharp need statement. Do not suggest a solution yet.

What is Define?

Define is where your team makes sense of everything learned during Empathy. You analyse interviews, maps and personas to frame one clear user need statement.

What students need to do

  • Review your Empathy Map, quotes and needs from Phase 01.
  • Build a User Persona using T05 from real evidence.
  • Cluster findings into insights and write a User Need Statement using T06.
  • Save both templates, pass the Define quiz, then submit to earn your badge.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Do not invent persona details — use real evidence.
  • Do not confuse a need with a solution.
  • Do not group notes by who said them — group by meaning.
  • Do not skip the insight: it explains why the need matters.

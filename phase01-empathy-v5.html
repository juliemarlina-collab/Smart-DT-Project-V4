PHASE 01 · EMPATHY

Understand users before you solve.

In this phase, your team observes, interviews, transcribes and maps real user needs before moving to Define.

Quick Info: What is POEMS?

POEMS helps beginners observe a real situation without jumping straight to solution. Look carefully at People, Objects, Environment, Messages and Services.

1. Observe first

Look at what is happening. Do not judge or solve too early.

2. Write evidence

Use short notes, user quotes, actions and things you can actually see.

3. Find patterns

After observing, discuss what problems appear more than once.

Beginner Sample: POEMS Observation

Background situation: Students are waiting at a counter to submit documents. The queue is unclear and some students look confused.

PPeople

Students, counter staff and friends waiting together. Some students keep asking others what to do.

OObjects

Queue number, phones, forms, pens, bag, notice board and counter table.

EEnvironment

Busy counter area, limited seats, students standing, unclear direction signs.

MMessages

Printed instruction, counter number, WhatsApp announcement and verbal reminders from staff.

SServices

Document checking, queue calling, asking questions, correcting incomplete forms.

Beginner reminder: POEMS is not the solution yet. It is only evidence collection. After this, use the evidence to build your Empathy Map.

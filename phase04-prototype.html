Phase 04 · Prototype

Build rough versions to test early and improve faster.

In this phase, your team turns the selected idea into a simple prototype, records each version, collects feedback and prepares evidence before the Test phase.

What is Prototype?

Prototype is where your team makes the chosen idea visible and testable. It does not need to be perfect. A rough paper sketch, simple digital mock-up or cardboard model is enough if it helps users react and give feedback.

What students need to do

  • Plan what to build, what tools are needed and who does each role.
  • Build Version 1 quickly using a low-fidelity method.
  • Ask teammates or early users to react to the prototype.
  • Record feedback and improvements in the Version Log.
  • Build Version 2 and upload photo/file evidence.
  • Complete the readiness checklist before Phase 05 Test.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Do not spend too long making the first prototype beautiful.
  • Do not build a full product when a simple sketch is enough.
  • Do not forget to record what changed between versions.
  • Do not build something that no longer matches your problem statement.

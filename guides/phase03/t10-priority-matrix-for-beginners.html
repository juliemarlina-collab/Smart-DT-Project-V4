T10

Priority Matrix Guide

Ideate | Day 1 | CAMP21 Smart DT Project
Purpose

Use the Impact Effort Priority Matrix to compare ideas and choose the strongest idea to bring into Prototype. This version uses four quadrants, not the old scoring table.

Beginner Rule

Choose ideas that create real impact and can realistically be done. High-impact ideas are exciting, but effort must be considered before prototyping.

How the matrix works

Quick / Easy Wins

High impact + low effort. These are strong ideas to try quickly.

Major Projects

High impact + high effort. Good ideas, but they need more resources or time.

Fill-In

Low impact + low effort. Easy to do, but not the main priority.

Thankless Tasks / Money Pit

Low impact + high effort. Usually avoid or simplify these ideas.

Same Case Study

Craft seller at a local craft area: Ideas should help attract young visitors, communicate the value of handmade crafts, and encourage purchases or repeat visits.

Example placement
  • Quick / Easy Win: QR product story cards with short videos.
  • Major Project: Full mobile craft trail app with rewards and map.
  • Fill-In: Update product labels with nicer colours.
  • Thankless Task / Money Pit: Build expensive digital kiosk with low visitor use.
How to complete T10
  1. List your best ideas from T07, T08 and T09.
  2. Decide whether each idea is high or low impact.
  3. Decide whether each idea is high or low effort.
  4. Place each idea into the correct quadrant.
  5. Select the best final idea and justify why it should move to Prototype.
Recommended final choice

Usually choose from Quick / Easy Wins or a realistic Major Project. Avoid ideas that are high effort but low impact.

T09

SCAMPER Guide

Ideate | Day 1 | CAMP21 Smart DT Project
Purpose

Use SCAMPER to improve one selected idea by changing, combining, adapting or rethinking it in different ways before final prioritisation.

Beginner Rule

Do not throw away the first idea too quickly. Improve it using different SCAMPER lenses first.

Same Case Study

Selected idea: Craft Story QR Display for a craft seller who wants young visitors to understand product value and return again.

SCAMPER prompts
S — Substitute

What can be replaced to make the idea easier to understand or use?

C — Combine

What can be combined to make the experience more helpful or attractive?

A — Adapt

What idea from another shop, market or service can be adapted here?

M — Modify

What can be enlarged, simplified, made more visual or improved?

P — Put to another use

How else can the product, display or space be used?

E — Eliminate

What can be removed to reduce confusion or effort?

R — Reverse / Rearrange

What can be reversed or rearranged to improve flow or attention?

Best SCAMPER idea

Choose the strongest improvement and explain why it is promising.

Example for Craft Seller
  • Substitute: Replace long written product descriptions with 30-second QR story videos.
  • Combine: Combine the QR story with a visitor stamp card.
  • Adapt: Adapt museum audio-guide style into a mini craft story tour.
  • Modify: Make product tags larger and more photo-friendly.
  • Put to another use: Use the display corner as a mini workshop booking point.
  • Eliminate: Remove too many technical craft terms.
  • Reverse: Let visitors choose a story first, then discover the product.
Output to bring into T10

Bring your improved idea and compare it with other ideas using the 4-quadrant priority matrix.

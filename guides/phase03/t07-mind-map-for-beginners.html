T07

Mind Map Guide

Ideate | Day 1 | CAMP21 Smart DT Project
Purpose

Use a mind map to organise many related ideas visually. Start from the central challenge, then connect ideas into themes, sub-ideas and possible solutions.

Beginner Rule

Do not judge ideas yet. Mind mapping is for exploring connections. Quantity comes before selection.

Same Case Study

Craft seller at a local craft area: A seller who makes handmade batik and woven products wants to attract more visitors and increase sales, especially among younger tourists and locals.

How to complete T07
1. Central challenge

Write the final user need statement from Define in the centre.

2. Add branches

Use the template themes: Customer/Visitor, Product/Craft, Promotion/Awareness, Shopping Experience, Payment/Access, New Idea/Opportunity.

3. Add many ideas

Write short idea keywords under each branch. Do not filter too early.

4. Circle the best idea

Choose one promising idea to develop further in T08 Sketching.

Example for Craft Seller
  • Customer / Visitor: youth craft trail, student visitor stamp card, guided craft story corner.
  • Product / Craft: mini batik kit, custom woven keychain, limited heritage collection.
  • Promotion / Awareness: TikTok craft story, QR product story, live demo schedule.
  • Shopping Experience: photo-friendly display, try-before-buy corner, workshop booking.
  • Payment / Access: e-wallet QR, bundle price, student discount.
  • New Opportunity: heritage passport challenge.
Output to bring into T08

Choose one idea from the mind map and prepare to sketch how it looks and works.

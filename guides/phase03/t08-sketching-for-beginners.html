T08

Sketching Guide

Ideate | Day 1 | CAMP21 Smart DT Project
Purpose

Use sketching to visualise your selected idea clearly through quick sketches, notes and key features. Sketches help your team explain how the idea works before building a prototype.

Beginner Rule

Make it clear, not perfect. A rough sketch is enough if it shows the user, main feature and step-by-step experience.

Same Case Study

Craft seller at a local craft area: The seller needs a way to attract young visitors and communicate the story and value of handmade crafts.

How to complete T08
1. Selected idea

Choose the best idea from T07 Mind Map.

2. Main sketch

Draw or describe the main view of the idea.

3. Detail sketches

Show front/main view, user interaction and key feature close-up.

4. Key features

List the important parts that make the idea useful.

5. How it works

Describe Step 1, Step 2 and Step 3 from the user's view.

6. Materials/tools

List simple materials or digital tools needed to build it.

Example for Craft Seller
  • Idea name: Craft Story QR Display.
  • Problem solved: Young visitors do not understand the meaning and value behind the craft.
  • Target user: Young tourists and local visitors.
  • Main sketch: A display table with QR codes beside batik/woven products.
  • How it works: Visitor scans QR → watches short story video → receives discount or stamp after learning.
  • Key features: QR story card, short video, product meaning, seller voice, loyalty stamp.
Output to bring into T09

Choose the sketch idea you want to improve using SCAMPER.

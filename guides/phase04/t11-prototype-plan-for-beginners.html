Prototype Direction Plan

Prototype  |  Phase 04  |  CAMP21 Smart DT Project
1 What is a prototype direction plan?

A prototype is a simple, testable version of your idea — not the final product. Before building, this plan decides what you'll build, who it's for, what it needs, and who on the team does each task. Plan first, build rough, test fast.

2 A prototype can be…
Pick the simplest format that lets you test your idea:
✏️

Sketch

Hand-drawn idea on paper.

🧱

Model

A rough physical build.

📱

Mock-up

A fake but realistic version.

🗂️

Wireframe

A screen/layout skeleton.

🎬

Simulation

Act out / role-play the experience.

🖼️

Poster

A visual board explaining it.

3 The 7 things to plan
1Selected ConceptWhat are you building or showing?
2Prototype FormatWhich format above fits best?
3Target UserWho will experience it?
4Key FeaturesWhat must it show or do?
5Materials / Tools / AIWhat do you need to build it?
6Team Task DistributionWho does what?
7Success CheckHow will you know it's ready?
RememberBuild only what you need to test the idea.

6 Beginner tips
1 Keep it low-fidelity — rough is fine.
2 Build only enough to test the idea.
3 Agree the success check before building.
4 A prototype is for learning, not perfection.

🧕🏽
Concept & format“Make-your-own batik” corner — a small model + poster.
Target userYoung visitors & students at the craft area.
Key featuresHands-on stamping table · QR story video · photo spot.
MaterialsCardboard, fabric scraps, stamps, printed poster, a phone.
Team tasksBuilder · poster designer · content writer · tester · pitcher.

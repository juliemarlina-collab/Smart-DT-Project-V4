Pitching Preparation

Prototype | Phase 04 | CAMP21 Smart DT Project
1 What is pitching preparation?

Pitching preparation helps your team explain the user problem, prototype solution, evidence and impact clearly for evaluation. It turns your Design Thinking work into a short, confident story.

2 Pitch rule

Show evidence, not just excitement. A strong pitch connects user needs to the prototype and explains why the idea matters.

3 The pitch structure
1
Hook

Start with user quote or problem.

2
User need

Explain who needs help and why.

3
Prototype

Show what you built and how it works.

4
Impact

Explain value, evidence and next step.

4 Evaluation prep checklist
Prototype readyCan judges see or try the prototype?
Evidence readyAre photos, links or files labelled?
Speaker roles readyDoes each member know their part?
Q&A readyCan the team answer why this idea matters?
5 Beginner tips
1 Keep it 2–3 minutes.
2 Use one clear user quote.
3 Show prototype evidence early.
4 End with impact and next step.
6 Case study — Craft Seller pitch
🧕🏽
Hook“Young people don't know why handmade crafts are valuable.”
User needThe craft seller needs a way to attract young visitors and explain craft stories.
PrototypeA make-your-own batik corner with QR story cards and photo spot.
ImpactVisitors learn, try, share and are more likely to purchase or return.
💡 Pitch focus: connect culture, user need, prototype and sustainability.
“ Tell the story. Show the evidence. Explain the impact. ”

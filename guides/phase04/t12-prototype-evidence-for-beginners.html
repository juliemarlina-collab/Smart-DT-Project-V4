Prototype Evidence

Prototype | Phase 04 | CAMP21 Smart DT Project
1 What is prototype evidence?

Prototype evidence is the proof that your team has built something testable. It can be a photo, video, sketch, poster, mock-up link, model image or screen recording.

2 Evidence rule

Make it clear enough for another person to understand. Evidence should show what was built, how it works and what changed between versions.

3 Accepted evidence formats
Photo evidencePrototype model, paper sketch, poster or display board.
Digital evidenceCanva, Figma, Google Drive, website, video or screen recording link.
Process evidenceVersion 1, Version 2, final version and readiness check.

5 Beginner tips
1 Use clear photos, not blurry ones.
2 Label the evidence link.
3 Show what changed between versions.
4 Keep access settings open for reviewers.

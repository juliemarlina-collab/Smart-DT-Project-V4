User Persona

Define | Phase 02 | CAMP21 Smart DT Project
1 What is a persona?

A persona is a believable profile of one representative user, built from your empathy evidence — not invented. It turns scattered findings into a single human your team can design for.

2 Real vs Invented

Evidence-based: Uses observed actions, quotes and repeated patterns.
Avoid: random age, guessed personality, or solution-first statements.

3 The persona snapshot
Name / Aliasa realistic name for your user
Age / Rangee.g. 18–22 or 40s
Role / Occupatione.g. craft seller, student
Craft / Heritage contexttheir cultural background
Location / Backgroundwhere they live or work
Quoteone real line that captures them

Define | Day 1 | CAMP21 Smart DT Project
1What is a user persona?

A persona is a clear profile of one important user group. It must be based on interview findings, observations, pain points, motivations and cultural context — not imagination.

2Persona Snapshot

👤 Name / Alias

A simple label for the user group.

📅 Age / Range

Use an approximate range where needed.

💼 Role / Occupation

What role do they play in the context?

💬 Quote

Choose a real quote that represents them.

3The 4 Core Areas

🎯 Goals & Motivations

What matters most to this persona?

⚠️ Pain Points

What blocks, frustrates or worries them?

💛 Needs

What support does the persona need?

🤝 Opportunity Area

Where can your team create impact?

4Case Study

Local craft seller: A seller who makes handmade batik and woven products wants to attract more visitors, but struggles with online promotion and explaining product value clearly.

Opportunity: Help the seller communicate product uniqueness, build visibility and encourage repeat customers.

User Insight & Needs

Define | Phase 02 | CAMP21 Smart DT Project
1 What is an insight & needs statement?

It turns many interview findings into clear insights and user needs. Raw quotes become something your team can act on without jumping to a solution.

2 The method
1
Gather

Write key quotes.

2
Group

Cluster notes.

3
Label

Name themes.

4
Formulate

Create statement.

3 Clustering: from notes to themes
Low Awareness
Visitors do not know the products.
Few visitors on weekdays.
Marketing
Hard to promote online.
Price & Value
Prices are compared with mass-produced items.
Loyalty
Need repeat customers.
4 The formula
[ User ] needs a way to [ Need ] because [ Insight ].
5 Insight vs Need

Insight: what you learned from evidence.
Need: what would help the user succeed.

6 Beginner tips
1 Listen more than you speak.
2 Group by meaning.
3 Avoid assumptions.
4 A need is not a solution.
7 Case study — Craft Seller
🧕🏽
HeardVisitors know very little about the products.
ThemesLow awareness, marketing, price and value.
InsightLow visibility and limited digital reach reduce sales.
NeedsAttract visitors and communicate value.
Statement: The craft seller needs a way to reach visitors because low visibility and weak digital presence limit steady sales.
“ Group by meaning. An insight is what you learned; a need is what helps. ”

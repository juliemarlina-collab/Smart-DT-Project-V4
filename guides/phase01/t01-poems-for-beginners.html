POEMS Observation for Beginners

Empathise | Day 1 | CAMP21 Smart DT Project
1What is POEMS?

POEMS is a simple checklist for observing a real situation. Instead of guessing, your team records what you can see across five lenses.

People · Objects · Environment · Messages · Services
2Observe vs Guess

✅ Observation

“Visitor reads the sign twice.”
“Three people wait near the table.”

❌ Opinion

“The sign is badly designed.”
“She is nervous.”

3The 5 Lenses

👥 People

Who is there? Roles, actions, interactions.

🧰 Objects

Tools, materials, products and items used.

🏠 Environment

Space, layout, movement and atmosphere.

💬 Messages

Signs, words, symbols or stories seen/heard.

🤝 Services

Systems or support around the experience.

4How to Use It
1

Choose

Pick one real place.

2

Watch

Observe quietly.

3

Record

Write facts in each box.

4

Spot Patterns

Find repeats and surprises.

5Case Study

Batik workshop visitor: The first-time visitor watches others before touching the cloth. This suggests beginners rely on watching before they dare to try.

“Observe first. Judge later.”

POEMS Observation
for Beginners

Empathise  |  Day 1  |  CAMP21 Smart DT Project
1 What is POEMS?

POEMS is a simple checklist for observing a real situation. Instead of guessing what is happening, you quietly record exactly what you see across five lenses — turning watching into structured evidence.

P · O · E · M · S = People · Objects · Environment · Messages · Services
2 Observe vs Guess
✅ Observation — write this
"Visitor reads the sign twice."
"Three people wait near the table."
"She watches before touching the cloth."
❌ Opinion — avoid this
"The sign is badly designed."
"It looks chaotic."
"She is nervous."

Rule: record what your eyes can prove. Save judgement for later.

3 The 5 Lenses
👥

P — People

Who is there? Roles, actions, emotions, interactions.

🧰

O — Objects

What tools, materials or products are used?

🏠

E — Environment

What is the space like? Layout, movement, atmosphere.

💬

M — Messages

What signs, words, symbols or stories are seen or heard?

🤝

S — Services

What systems or services support the experience?

4 How to use it
1
Choose

Pick one real place to observe.

›
2
Watch

Observe quietly for a set time. Don't interrupt.

›
3
Record

Write what you see in each POEMS box — facts, not opinions.

›
4
Spot patterns

Note what repeats and what surprised you.

5 Beginner tips
1 Write facts, not feelings.
2 Use your eyes first — observe before you ask.
3 Sketch or photograph if permission is given.
4 One detail can sit in two lenses — that's fine.
6 Case study — Batik Workshop Visitor
🧕🏽
PeopleInstructor demonstrates; first-time visitor watches; two friends nearby.
ObjectsWax pen (canting), cloth, dye pots, stove, samples.
EnvironmentWarm, busy studio; tools shared on one table.
MessagesHandwritten price list; "don't touch wet cloth" sign.
ServicesPayment counter; one-to-one help on request.
💡 Insight: First-timers rely on watching others before they dare to try.

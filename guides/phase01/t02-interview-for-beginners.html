Interview Guide
for Beginners

Empathise  |  Day 1  |  CAMP21 Smart DT Project
1 What is an interview guide?

An interview guide helps your team ask the right questions, listen carefully, and collect useful stories, actions, thoughts and feelings from users. It keeps the interview focused while still allowing natural conversation.

2 What to prepare
🎯

Goal

Know what you want to learn.

💬

Questions

Prepare open-ended questions.

👥

Roles

One asks, one notes if in pairs.

📓

Materials

Notebook, consent, evidence if allowed.

3 Interview flow
1
Introduce Yourself

Greet the user and share who you are.

›
2
Introduce Project

Explain it and why their input matters.

›
3
Build Rapport

Show respect and create trust.

›
4
Ask for Stories

Invite experiences and examples.

›
5
Explore Emotions

Ask how they felt and what mattered.

›
6
Follow-Up + Wrap

Clarify, thank them, close well.

4 Beginner tips
1 Ask open-ended questions.
2 Start simple to break the ice.
3 Let the user tell stories.
4 Watch body language and tone.
5 Record exact words when possible.
6 Unpack notes soon after.
5 Sample question starters
❝Can you tell me about your experience…?
❝What usually happens when you…?
❝How did you feel at that moment?
❝Why was that important to you?
6 Case study — First-time Batik Workshop Visitor
🧕🏽SituationA student interviews a visitor learning batik for the first time.
💬Good questions"What interested you about batik?" · "What part feels difficult?"
👁️What we observeThe visitor asks for help, watches closely, hesitates before trying.
⭐Key insightBeginners need clear steps, reassurance and hands-on guidance.
👂 Reminder: Listen more than you speak — the goal is to understand the user, not to test your own ideas.
“ Listen more than you speak. ”

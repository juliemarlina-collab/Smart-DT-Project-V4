Empathy Map
for Beginners

Empathise | Day 1 | CAMP21 Smart DT Project
1What is an empathy map?

An empathy map helps your team organise interview and observation findings to understand what users say, do, think and feel. It turns scattered notes into a clearer picture of one person.

2The 4 Main Areas

💬 Say

Exact quotes, key phrases or important words from the user.

🏃 Do

Actions, behaviours, habits or body language you observed.

💭 Think

Possible beliefs, worries or hidden concerns.

❤️ Feel

Emotions such as frustration, excitement, fear, hope or confusion.

3How to Use It
1

Listen

Review interviews, observations and transcription.

2

Sort

Place evidence into Say, Do, Think and Feel.

3

Find Patterns

Look for pains, needs and surprises.

4

Use It

Write a clear user need for Define phase.

4Beginner Tips
1

Use exact quotes for SAY.

2

Use real observation for DO.

3

Use careful inference for THINK and FEEL.

4

Focus on patterns, not random details.

5Case Study — Batik Workshop Visitor

Say

“I want to try, but I’m scared I’ll spoil the cloth.”

Do

Watches closely, takes photos, asks a friend and hesitates.

Think

“I need simple steps and reassurance before I begin.”

Feel

Curious, excited, nervous and slightly overwhelmed.

💡 Insight: Beginners need step-by-step guidance, reassurance, and a safe space to try.
6Bridge to Define

User need statement: The first-time visitor needs reassurance and clear step-by-step guidance before trying batik.

Reminder: A need is a verb or condition, not a solution. Write “needs to feel safe trying,” not “needs an app.”

“Map the person, not just the problem.”

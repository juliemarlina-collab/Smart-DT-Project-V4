1 What is an empathy map?

An empathy map helps your team organise interview findings to understand what users say, do, think and feel — turning scattered notes into a clear picture of one person.

💬SAY
💭THINK
🏃DO
❤️FEEL
🙂
2 The 4 main areas
💬

Say

Exact quotes, key phrases or important words from the interview.

🏃

Do

Actions, behaviours, habits or body language you observed.

💭

Think

Possible beliefs, worries or hidden concerns — inferred.

❤️

Feel

Emotions: frustration, excitement, fear, hope or confusion.

3 How to use it
1
Listen

Review the interview or your observations.

›
2
Sort

Split notes into Say, Do, Think and Feel.

›
3
Find patterns

Look for pains, needs and surprises.

›
4
Use it

Build a persona and define the problem.

4 Beginner tips
1 Use exact quotes for SAY.
2 Use real observations for DO.
3 Use careful inference for THINK & FEEL.
4 Focus on patterns, not random details.
5 Case study — Batik Workshop Visitor
🧕🏽
Say:"I want to try, but I'm scared I'll spoil the cloth."
Do:Watches closely, takes photos, asks a friend, hesitates.
Think:"I need simple steps and reassurance before I begin."
Feel:Curious, excited, nervous, slightly overwhelmed.
💡 Insight: Beginners need step-by-step guidance, reassurance, and a safe space to try.
“ Map the person, not just the problem. ”

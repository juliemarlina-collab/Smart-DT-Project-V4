1 What is transcription?

Transcription turns your recorded interview into written words you can read, highlight and share as evidence. It's how a conversation becomes proof of what the user really said — 🎙️ → 📄.

2 Clean vs Raw
🎙️ Raw — what you hear
"Erm, I really wanted to try but, like, I was scared I'd, you know, spoil the cloth lah."
✨ Clean — what you keep
"I wanted to try, but I was scared I'd spoil the cloth."

Rule: tidy the words, never change the meaning. Keep key quotes word-for-word.

3 What goes into a transcript
🎧

Audio Info

File name, length, who was interviewed, where.

🏷️

Speaker Labels

Mark who is talking — Interviewer / Visitor / Artisan.

📄

Clean Transcript

The conversation written out tidily.

❝

Key Quotes

The strongest exact words, saved as evidence.

4 How to transcribe — step by step

›

5 Beginner tips
1 Always ask permission before recording (consent).
2 A phone or free AI tool can auto-transcribe — then tidy it by hand.
3 Keep important quotes word-for-word; don't paraphrase those.
4 Note tone in brackets: [laughs], [pauses], [sounds unsure].
5 Transcribe soon after, while it's fresh.

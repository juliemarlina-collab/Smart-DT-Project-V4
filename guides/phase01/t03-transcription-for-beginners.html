Transcription
for Beginners

Empathise | Day 1 | CAMP21 Smart DT Project
1What is transcription?

Transcription turns your recorded interview into written words you can read, highlight and share as evidence. It helps your team keep the user's real voice before moving to the Empathy Map.

2Clean vs Raw

🎙️ Raw — what you hear

“Erm, I really wanted to try but, like, I was scared I’d spoil the cloth lah.”

✨ Clean — what you keep

“I wanted to try, but I was scared I’d spoil the cloth.”

Rule: tidy the words, but never change the meaning. Keep strong quotes word-for-word.

3What Goes into a Transcript?

🎧 Audio Info

File name, length, who was interviewed and where.

🏷️ Speaker Labels

Interviewer / Visitor / Artisan / Facilitator.

📄 Clean Transcript

The conversation written out tidily.

❝ Key Quotes

The strongest exact words saved as evidence.

4How to Transcribe — Step by Step
1

Listen

Play the full recording once from start to end.

2

Write

Type what is said, line by line.

3

Clean

Remove “um”, repeats and false starts — keep the meaning.

4

Mark

Highlight the strongest quotes and what they reveal.

5Beginner Tips
1

Ask permission before recording.

2

Use AI transcription, then check manually.

3

Keep important quotes word-for-word.

4

Note tone: [laughs], [pauses], [sounds unsure].

6Case Study — Batik Workshop Visitor

Raw

“Erm, I really wanted to try but… I was scared I’d spoil the cloth.”

Clean

“I wanted to try, but I was scared I’d spoil the cloth.”

Key Quote

“I was scared I’d spoil the cloth.”

Meaning

Beginners fear mistakes. They need reassurance and a safe first try.

💡 Insight: The quote shows fear of failure, not lack of interest.

“Keep their words. Lose the noise.”

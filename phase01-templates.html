T01 Interview Guide

Prepare and conduct interviews with at least 3 real target users.

Not Started
A1 Interactive Activity
  • Before: decide the user group, location, and 5 open-ended questions.
  • During: listen actively, ask follow-up questions, and avoid suggesting answers.
  • After: record 3 key insights while the discussion is fresh.

Sample Interview Context

Target user: Student who often buys lunch at the canteen.
Good question: “Tell me about your experience during peak lunch time.”
Avoid yes/no questions. Use prompts such as “Why?”, “Can you explain more?”, and “What happened next?”

T02 Empathy Map

Organise findings into Says, Thinks, Does and Feels.

Not Started
A2 4-Quadrant Mapping
  • SAYS: write direct quotes from interviews.
  • THINKS: infer thoughts based on behaviour and responses.
  • DOES & FEELS: capture actions and emotions to reveal pains and gains.

T03 Persona

Build a simple user persona based on real empathy findings.

Not Started
A3 Build Persona
  • Profile: describe the user background.
  • Goals: explain what the user wants to achieve.
  • Pains: identify barriers, frustrations, or difficulties.

T04 Needs Summary

Summarise the main user needs before moving to Define.

Not Started
A4 Summarise Needs
  • Problem: what issue appears repeatedly?
  • Need: what does the user truly need?
  • Evidence: which quotes or observations support this?

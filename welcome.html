About Smart DT Project

Smart DT Project is a mobile learning companion for Final Year Project students. It guides you through the Design Thinking process step by step, helping your team understand users, define problems, build ideas, prototype solutions, and test improvements with evidence.

What to expect

Inside the app, you will be guided step by step so you always know what to do next.

  • 5 Design Thinking phases from Empathy to Test, organised in a clear learning path.
  • Quick info and quizzes to help you understand each phase before completing templates.
  • Digital templates and evidence to record your interviews, ideas, prototype progress, and testing feedback.
  • Progress and supervisor gates so your team can move forward with better direction and approval.
WELCOME NOTE

Take your time and follow each phase properly. The goal is not to rush to a solution, but to understand real users, define the right problem, create meaningful ideas, and improve through feedback.

Think

Understand people, problems, and project direction clearly.

Test

Validate ideas through real evidence and user feedback.

Improve

Refine your solution and grow as a project team.
